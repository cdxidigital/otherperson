# EPISODE 0 — PROLOGUE
**Show:** The Other Person in the Room  
**Host:** Adam James  
**Guest:** Sarah  
**Runtime target:** 8–10 minutes  
**Voices in the room:** Adam, Sarah  
**Tone:** Two friends at a table. The mic is already rolling. Nobody is performing a podcast yet. Interrupt. Bicker. Land.

---

**CONTENT NOTE** *(spoken, not announced)*

ADAM: Hang on — before you start roasting me — content note. This series talks about mental illness, trauma, addiction, grief, panic, body stuff, low mood. No graphic detail. Skip if you need to. Numbers are in the show notes.

SARAH: You always do the safety spiel like you're about to detonate something.

ADAM: I *am* about to detonate something.

SARAH: You're about to interview my anxiety.

ADAM: Yes.

SARAH: On purpose.

ADAM: On purpose.

SARAH: I hate this already and we haven't even started.

---

**THE CHAIR**

ADAM: You can hate it after the chair.

SARAH: Oh my god. You actually did it. You put a chair there.

ADAM: You told me to.

SARAH: I was being funny.

ADAM: You were being honest. That's worse.

SARAH: There's a difference.

ADAM: Not in your kitchen, there's not.

SARAH: Don't look at it.

ADAM: I'm looking at it.

SARAH: It loves that.

ADAM: It's a chair, Sarah.

SARAH: That's what you think.

---

**WHO WE ARE**

ADAM: Okay. For the people who just arrived. I'm Adam James. This is The Other Person in the Room. Sarah's been my friend for years. She takes her tea too weak. She remembers everyone's birthday. She cancels more than she shows up and then apologises like she committed a crime.

SARAH: I *did* commit a crime. I committed the crime of having a nervous system.

ADAM: That's not a crime.

SARAH: Tell that to the clipboard.

ADAM: We'll get to the clipboard. That's literally the show.

SARAH: Hi. I'm Sarah. Apparently my anxiety is sitting behind me, slightly to the left, holding a clipboard, looking disappointed that I still haven't double-checked the stove. I hate that sentence. I also said it. So.

ADAM: That's the sentence. That's why we're here.

SARAH: Which one.

ADAM: The clipboard one.

SARAH: Yeah. Because if I could see its face properly I'd punch it. And it knows that. That's why it sits behind me. It's a coward. A very organised coward.

ADAM: With stationery.

SARAH: With *branded* stationery. Don't give it a logo. It would unionise.

---

**THE STUPID DISCOVERY**

ADAM: There's something deeply stupid about realising someone you've known for years has been living with a person you've never met.

SARAH: It's not a person.

ADAM: It has a clipboard, Sarah.

SARAH: Okay. Fair. It has a clipboard.

ADAM: You know someone's laugh. You know how they take their coffee. You know the version that answers the phone and says I'm fine with exactly enough conviction to make you stop asking.

SARAH: That's a very specific accusation.

ADAM: It is. I've been that idiot. I've said just come anyway. I've treated cancelled plans like a personality flaw. I'm putting that on the record because if this show pretends I was always the wise friend, it's a lie.

SARAH: You were not the wise friend.

ADAM: I was the guy with a group chat and no patience.

SARAH: You brought dip though.

ADAM: I brought dip.

SARAH: Dip is not emotional intelligence.

ADAM: Dip is dip. Let me have this.

---

**WHAT THE SHOW IS**

ADAM: Most podcasts about mental illness ask: what's wrong with you.

SARAH: Charming.

ADAM: This one asks: what is it like to live with you. Then it goes one step further. I interview the person. Then I interview the thing they live with.

SARAH: You're going to talk to the chair.

ADAM: I'm going to talk to the chair.

SARAH: It's going to love that.

ADAM: An illness can't sit in a chair. It can't roll its eyes. But you lot describe these things like roommates. Anxiety warns. Depression negotiates. Trauma keeps receipts. OCD runs dress rehearsals. Addiction knocks like an ex who still has a key.

SARAH: Mine thinks it's middle management.

ADAM: Yours *is* middle management. Marcus's is a landlord. Elena's is a security guard who never clocks off. We'll get there.

SARAH: Don't make me the inspirational one.

ADAM: You're employed by a clipboard. That's the joke. That's also the tragedy.

SARAH: Please don't say tragedy on the first page. You'll scare the neighbours.

ADAM: The neighbours already think we're recording a cult.

SARAH: We might be.

---

**THE DEAL**

ADAM: The humour is here because the situation is absurd. The hard bits are here because the cost is real. This is not a smear campaign. It's the side of life we usually only sense. The arguments people have with the voice that never leaves. The jokes they make about their own destruction because the alternative is screaming.

SARAH: Don't say destruction.

ADAM: Fine. Their own overtime.

SARAH: Better.

ADAM: Every person in this series can refuse a question, pause, stop, or pull material. Several will. Those refusals stay in. If an episode is too much, leave. Come back. Or don't. The point is not endurance. The point is introduction.

SARAH: And if my clipboard tries to take over the episode—

ADAM: I have your permission to tell it to sit down.

SARAH: It won't listen.

ADAM: That's also the show.

SARAH: Everybody has something sitting in the room with them.

ADAM: Don't you start being the tagline.

SARAH: You were going to say it.

ADAM: I was going to say it later. With music.

SARAH: There's no music. There's a dog on my foot and cold tea.

ADAM: That's the production value.

SARAH: I want a raise.

ADAM: Talk to the clipboard.

SARAH: I hate you.

ADAM: Episode one. Sarah. And the clipboard.

SARAH: It's already taking notes.

ADAM: Of course it is.

---

**END OF PROLOGUE**
