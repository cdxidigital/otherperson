# Episodes 0 & 1 — Organic shooting scripts
Host: Adam James. Three-voice. Interrupt, argue, land.

---

## EP 0 — Prologue (Adam + Sarah)

ADAM: Okay wait, before we even start, content note, this whole series talks about mental illness, trauma, addiction, grief, panic, all of it. No graphic stuff. Skip if you need to. Numbers are in the show notes.

SARAH: You always do the safety spiel like you're about to detonate something.

ADAM: I am about to detonate something. I'm about to interview your anxiety.

SARAH: Oh my god. You actually did it. You put a chair there.

ADAM: You told me to.

SARAH: I was being funny.

ADAM: You were being honest. That's worse.

SARAH: Hi. I'm Sarah. Apparently my anxiety is sitting behind me with a clipboard. I hate this already.

ADAM: I'm Adam James. This is The Other Person in the Room. And the whole idea started because of that sentence you just said.

SARAH: Which one.

ADAM: The clipboard one.

SARAH: Yeah. Because if I could see its face I'd punch it. And it knows that. That's why it sits behind me. It's a coward. A very organised coward.

ADAM: That's the show. I've known you for years. What I didn't know is you've been living with a person I have never met.

SARAH: It's not a person.

ADAM: It has a clipboard, Sarah.

SARAH: Okay. Fair. It has a clipboard.

…everybody has something sitting in the room with them…

SARAH: Don't make me the inspirational one.

ADAM: You're employed by a clipboard. That's the joke. That's also the tragedy.

## EP 1 — Sarah + Anxiety (The Clipboard)

Three voices. Anxiety never takes leave. Sarah is staff. They argue.

ANXIETY: Certainty. Full. Laminated. Preferably notarised.

ADAM: Yes.

ANXIETY: Rude.

ANXIETY: You are staff. Job title: prevent catastrophe. KPIs: impossible.

SARAH: I didn't apply.

ANXIETY: You stayed. That's an application.

SARAH: You were being an eleven year old's nervous system with a God complex.

ANXIETY: The building looked flammable.

SARAH: It was a school hall.

ANXIETY: Exactly. Have you seen school halls.

ANXIETY: You don't get a medal for not burning the house down.

SARAH: That's my daily affirmation.

SARAH: I don't want it gone. I want it quieter.

ANXIETY: I'll take it under advisement. No promises.

SARAH: See. That's a no.

ANXIETY: That's a maybe. Maybe is how we survive.

ANXIETY: She is still here. You're welcome.

SARAH: I am still here. I just want to be here without the overtime.
