DEPRESSION: I'm almost merciful. In a completely unhelpful way. That's the brand.

ADAM: What should people do when you go quiet.

MARCUS: Stay. Don't invent hobbies. Caring feels heavier than I can lift. It isn't that I don't.

ADAM: Stay anyway.

ADAM: The landlord is still collecting rent. Marcus is still here. Next: Elena. A guard who will not clock off.
