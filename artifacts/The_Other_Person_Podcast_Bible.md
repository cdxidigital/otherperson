# THE OTHER PERSON IN THE ROOM
## Podcast Series Bible

**Working Title:** The Other Person in the Room  
**Tagline:** Everybody has something sitting in the room with them.  
**Format:** Narrative interview podcast  
**Host:** Adam James  
**Season Structure:** 1 season • 14 episodes (Prologue + 12 interviews + Finale)  
**Hard cap:** Every episode is **no more than 15 minutes**.  
**Tone:** Funny until it isn’t. Hard-hitting without spectacle. Intimate and human — *Nanette* energy in a short-form interview.

---

## SERIES OVERVIEW

Most podcasts about mental health interview the person *or* explain the diagnosis.  
This series does both at once — and then goes one step further.

Each episode features three voices in the same room:

1. **The Person** — the friend  
2. **The Illness** — a literary reconstruction given a voice, built only from how the participant described it  
3. **The Interviewer** — Adam James

The illness is never a monster. It is a difficult roommate with strong opinions, selective memory, and terrible timing. The humour sits next to the cost. That is the point.

---

## 15-MINUTE CLOCK

Use this structure on every episode. Do not overrun.

| Time | Section | Length |
|------|---------|--------|
| 0:00–0:40 | Title + spoken content note | ~40 sec |
| 0:40–2:00 | Host open / the room | ~1 min 20 |
| 2:00–11:00 | The conversation (Person + Interviewer) | ~9 min |
| 11:00–13:30 | The illness speaks (third voice) | ~2 min 30 |
| 13:30–15:00 | Afterward + close | ~1 min 30 |

**Edit rule:** If the raw interview runs long, cut to the sharpest exchange, one origin beat, one cost beat, one “what I wish you knew” beat, then the illness. Leave silences. Cut repetition.

Prologue and Finale also cap at 15 minutes.

---

## CORE PRODUCTION RULES

- Illness voice: closer mic, drier delivery, never cartoonish.
- Leave the silences in.
- Content notes spoken at the top and written in show notes.
- No methods. No graphic detail. No turning crisis into entertainment.
- Music: sparse, human, slightly imperfect. Sting only at open and close.
- Full transcripts published with every episode.

## EPISODE LIST

All runtimes: **12–15 minutes. Hard stop at 15:00.**

### Episode 0 — Prologue
**Title:** The Other Person in the Room  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  

Adam James sets the premise in under two minutes. The possessive language. The decision to interview the illness as well as the person. Ends on Sarah’s first line about the clipboard.

**Key moment:** “Mine would be sitting behind me. Slightly to the left. Holding a clipboard.”

---

### Episode 1 — Sarah + Anxiety
**Title:** The Clipboard  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Chronic anxiety, hypervigilance, constant risk assessment.

Anxiety is a passive-aggressive project manager who never takes leave. It calls her staff. It keeps receipts for the times it was useful and deletes the rest.

**Illness personality:** Bureaucratic, disappointed, data-driven.  
**Signature line:** “You don’t get a medal for not burning the house down.”

**15-min cut:** camp / first clipboard → daily checking → “staff” exchange → what she wants (quieter, not gone).

---

### Episode 2 — Marcus + Depression
**Title:** The Landlord  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Major depression, emotional numbness, isolation, persistent low mood.

Depression sits like a landlord who has given up on renovations. It is polite about the demolition. That is the worst part.

**Illness personality:** Weary, proprietary, almost merciful in a completely unhelpful way.  
**Signature line:** “I’m almost merciful. In a completely unhelpful way. That’s my brand.”

**15-min cut:** the kitchen after his father left → the cover story of “tired” → the explanation it offers → stay anyway.

---

### Episode 3 — Elena + Trauma
**Title:** The Security Guard Who Never Clocks Off  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Complex trauma, PTSD, hypervigilance, body memory. No graphic descriptions of events.

Trauma is a security guard who never sits down. It laminates the one real incident and deletes the ninety-nine false alarms.

**Illness personality:** Alert, selective, loyal in the most exhausting way.  
**Signature line:** “People who say ‘I’m still here’ are usually about to stop checking.”

**15-min cut:** the body remembering first → the name arriving → one hour off the perimeter.

---

### Episode 4 — David + Bipolar
**Title:** The Weather  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Bipolar mood states, mania, depressive crashes.

Two people sharing one chair and arguing about the thermostat. Mood stabilisers narrowed the range. He misses the high. He does not miss the crash.

**Illness personality:** Dual, voltage-driven, unapologetic.  
**Signature line:** “You don’t apologise for electricity.”

**15-min cut:** festival vs blackout → the trade → love the weather, not just the sun.

---

### Episode 5 — Priya + OCD
**Title:** The Endless Play  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Obsessive-compulsive patterns, compulsory sequences, cost of rituals.

An exhausted director holding a script that grows new pages while you watch. Relief lasts about forty seconds.

**Illness personality:** Precise, relentless about completeness.  
**Signature line:** “Forty seconds of safety is still safety. You’re welcome.”

**15-min cut:** the sequence → the forty seconds → reliability that looks different.

---

### Episode 6 — Tom + Addiction
**Title:** The Ex Who Still Has a Key  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Alcohol addiction, bargaining, ongoing recovery. No detailed accounts of using.

Charismatic. Remembers every good night and none of the damage. Still tries the door.

**Illness personality:** Charming, patient, excellent timing.  
**Signature line:** “I can wait. I am extremely patient. And I have excellent timing.”

**15-min cut:** the lease rewrite → the Tuesdays → celebrate the boring parts.

---

### Episode 7 — Aisha + Panic
**Title:** National Emergency  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Panic attacks, sudden bodily emergency responses, false alarms that still feel life-threatening.

A slightly elevated heart rate treated like the building is on fire. It does not do “maybe later.”

**Illness personality:** Loud, certain, no proportion.  
**Signature line:** “I will finish when I finish. Your schedule is not my problem.”

**15-min cut:** supermarket aisle → don’t say calm down → presence without matching panic.

---

### Episode 8 — Liam + Rejection Sensitivity
**Title:** Advanced Pattern Recognition  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** ADHD-related rejection sensitivity, intense responses to perceived criticism or abandonment.

“I need a bit of space” arrives as “I am leaving forever.” It means well in the most relationship-destroying way possible.

**Illness personality:** Fast, scanning, defensive-protective.  
**Signature line:** “I call it advanced pattern recognition with a strong bias toward survival.”

**15-min cut:** the mistranslation → one ruined almost-exit → translate gently.

---

### Episode 9 — Sophie + Dysmorphia
**Title:** The Hostile Witness  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Body dysmorphia, negative body image, history of disordered eating. No behaviours or numbers.

The mirror has never been neutral. It has always been a hostile witness with excellent lighting.

**Illness personality:** Precise, critical, convinced it is the last honest voice.  
**Signature line:** “The measurements do not lie. Kindness does.”

**15-min cut:** the witness → the decade sentence → silence as care.

---

### Episode 10 — Jordan + Grief
**Title:** The Space That Won’t Vacate  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Complicated grief following the death of a sibling, ongoing absence.

Grief sits in the exact space his brother used to occupy. He would not ask it to leave. That would be another death.

**Illness personality:** Quiet, heavy, permanent.  
**Signature line:** “I do not leave. That is the entire job description.”

**15-min cut:** the broken calendar → no deadline → sit with me.

---

### Episode 11 — Mei + Social Anxiety
**Title:** The Thermostat  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Social anxiety, fear of judgement, cost of ordinary social interaction.

Every sentence is a potential exit wound. Silence is often how she stays in the room.

**Illness personality:** Calculating, safety-through-silence.  
**Signature line:** “Better to say nothing than to say the thing that makes them leave.”

**15-min cut:** the room temperature → the quiet as staying → thank you for staying too.

---

### Episode 12 — Alex + The Low Voice
**Title:** The Question That Stays Open  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  
**Content note:** Persistent depression and passive suicidal ideation. No plans, methods, or active intent. Focus on the daily decision to stay.

Not theatrical. Does not need volume. Keeps the question open like a radio that never quite goes off air.

**Illness personality:** Quiet, persistent, consistent.  
**Signature line:** “He answers the question every day by remaining. The answer is his. The question remains mine.”

**15-min cut:** the open question → “you just have to stay” → presence without pressure.

---

### Episode 13 — Finale
**Title:** The Other Person Was Me  
**Runtime:** 12–15 min  

Adam James turns the lens. Fear. Grief. Shame. The rooms he never introduced. One line from each of the twelve, then the close:

The other person may always be in the room.  
But they don’t necessarily get to decide who lives there.

## SERIES CLOSE

The illnesses may occupy rooms.  
They may rearrange the furniture.  
They may turn the lights off.  
They may sometimes convince the person there is no door.

But they are not the whole house.

A diagnosis can describe a person’s experience without becoming their identity.

The other person may always be in the room.  
But they don’t necessarily get to decide who lives there.
